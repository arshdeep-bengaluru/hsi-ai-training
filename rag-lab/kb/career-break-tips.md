# Tips for returning after a career break

SYNTHETIC training document written for this workshop; general advice, not an official HSI document.

## Frame your break honestly

State the break plainly on your resume and highlight what you did: volunteering, freelance work, caring responsibilities, courses.

## Refresh your skills

Pick one skill relevant to the role you want and finish a short course or project. HSI's training programs are one option.

## Update your LinkedIn profile

Add a clear headline, a short summary and your recent learning. Let recruiters see that you are ready to return.

## Use mentors and community

Join Mentor Circles, ask mentors for resume feedback and practise interviews with them.
