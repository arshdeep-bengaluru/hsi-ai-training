# Training and upskilling programs

SYNTHETIC training document based on public information from hersecondinnings.com. Not an official HSI document.

## Technology Orientation Training

Orientation tracks for women returning to technology: RPA, Salesforce, Python, IT Refresher, Redhat and Patent Agent.

## IT Comeback: Become a Developer

A program for women returning to technology who want to become developers.

## HSI Career Restart Program

An upskilling program for women restarting their careers after a break.

## Java Full Stack Returnship

A returnship focused on Java full stack development, combining training with a project opportunity.

## AI developer program

An upskilling program, run with partners (TNSDC and womenonbreak), that trains women on a career break to become AI developers.
