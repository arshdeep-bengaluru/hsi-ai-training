# Candidate onboarding guide

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. All details below are invented for the workshop and are NOT real HSI policy.

## Who this guide is for

This guide walks a new candidate through her first weeks with HSI, step by step. Following the steps in order helps the HSI team understand her goals and find the right opportunities quickly.

## Step 1: Create your account

Register on the HSI website with your email address. Confirm the email, then sign in to the candidate portal. Registration takes about five minutes.

## Step 2: Complete your profile

Fill in your education, your work history and the length of your career break. Describe what you did during the break, including volunteering, freelance work, caring responsibilities and courses. Upload your latest resume if you have one. Profiles with clear skills and recent learning are easier to match with employers.

## Step 3: Take the skills assessment

After your profile is complete, you take an online assessment. It checks technical and general skills relevant to the roles you are interested in. The assessment helps you and your coach decide where to focus. It is not a pass or fail test.

## Step 4: Meet your career coach

You are offered a first conversation with a career coach. In this session you talk about your goals, your timeline and any worries about returning. The coach suggests a plan, which may include a training track, resume support or interview practice.

## Step 5: Build your resume and LinkedIn profile

With the coach's help, rewrite your resume so that the career break is stated honestly and your strengths come first. Create or update your LinkedIn profile with a clear headline and a short summary.

## Step 6: Start applying and join the community

Browse the jobs and returnship listings and apply to the ones that fit. Join Mentor Circles and online events to meet other returners and mentors. Keep your profile up to date as you learn new skills.

## Tips for a smooth start

- Complete the steps in order; each one prepares the next.
- Keep your contact details current so the team can reach you.
- Tell your coach early if your plans or availability change.
- Use the helpdesk for account problems such as sign-in issues.
