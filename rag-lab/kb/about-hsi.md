# About Her Second Innings (HSI)

SYNTHETIC training document based on public information from hersecondinnings.com. Not an official HSI document.

Her Second Innings (HSI) supports women returning to work after a career break. Its mission is to empower and support women in their professional journeys: returning to work, projects, job placement and upskilling. HSI helps women achieve financial independence and social empowerment, and it puts employers in touch with job-ready women candidates.

HSI serves women returners, including those with engineering or IT backgrounds, as well as employers, mentors and recruitment partners.

Registration, job assistance and online events are free for candidates. Coaching, training and exclusive resources carry a small fee.

HSI is run by World of Opportunities for Women LLP (placement and training) together with the WOW Foundation (community and CSR-funded programs). The team is based in Bangalore.
