# Employer partnership guide

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. All details below are invented for the workshop and are NOT real HSI policy.

## Why this guide exists

This guide explains how an employer can host a returnship project with HSI, from the first conversation to the final review. It helps hiring managers prepare so that the participant and the team get the most from the project.

## Before the project starts

An employer should prepare the following before a returnship project begins:

- **A clear scope.** Describe the project in one page: the goal, the main deliverables, the tools and technologies, and how success will be judged.
- **A named hiring manager.** One person is responsible for weekly goals and feedback.
- **A workspace and access plan.** Decide which systems, accounts and tools the participant needs and who will approve access on the first day.
- **A buddy.** A teammate who answers day-to-day questions and helps the participant meet the team.

## Selecting a participant

The resourcing specialist shares a shortlist of participants whose skills match the project scope. The hiring manager reviews the profiles and holds a short interview with each shortlisted participant. HSI only shares a participant's profile with an employer after the participant has agreed.

## The first week

On the first day the hiring manager walks the participant through the project scope, the team and the weekly rhythm. By the end of the first week the participant should have working access to all required tools and a plan for the first two weeks of delivery.

## During the project

The hiring manager sets goals every Monday and gives written feedback every Friday. A monthly review with the career coach checks progress and removes blockers. Employers are encouraged to give honest, specific feedback; participants value it more than general praise.

## Mentoring and support

HSI assigns a mentor to each participant. The mentor is independent of the employer and offers advice and encouragement. Employers should allow participants time to meet their mentor each week.

## The final review

In the last two weeks the hiring manager, the participant and the career coach hold a review meeting. The employer decides whether to offer a continuing role, extend the project, or provide a reference. Whatever the outcome, the employer shares written feedback so the participant has a clear record of her work.

## Contact

Employers with questions about partnering should contact HSI through the helpdesk or the employer registration page.
