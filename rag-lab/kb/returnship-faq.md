# Returnship FAQ

SYNTHETIC training document based on public information from hersecondinnings.com. Not an official HSI document.

## Who can join a returnship?

Returnships are open to women who have taken a career break. This includes women from engineering and IT backgrounds as well as other professions.

## What is a returnship?

A returnship is a structured project or job opportunity that helps a woman restart her career after a break, with support from HSI and a hiring company.

## How do I apply for a returnship?

Register on the HSI website, complete your profile, and look at the returnship and job listings on the jobs page. Registration is free.

## Does HSI help with resumes?

Yes. HSI offers resume and LinkedIn guidance and job search tips as part of its career guidance services.
