# Services for employers

SYNTHETIC training document based on public information from hersecondinnings.com. Not an official HSI document.

## Assessment and Training

More than 100 technical and personality assessments, with proctoring options.

## Staffing Solutions

Temporary staffing where temporary workers are on HSI's payroll. HSI handles statutory compliance, including EPF and ESIC.

## Diversity Hiring Platform

Hiring strategy design and pre-screened shortlists of women candidates. HSI states that this reduces hiring cycle time by about 70 percent (a self-reported claim, to be verified).

## Consulting Services

IT development and automation projects (in partnership with UiPath), plus administration, marketing and sales projects.

## Posting a job

Employers can register on the HSI website, post jobs and review applicants.
