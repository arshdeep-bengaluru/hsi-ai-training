# Returnship program handbook

## Purpose of this handbook

This handbook describes how a typical returnship runs from the first week to the last. It is written for participants, mentors and hiring managers so that everyone shares the same expectations.

## The four phases

A returnship has four phases and lasts sixteen weeks in total.

### Phase 1: Prepare (weeks 1 and 2)

The participant completes her profile, takes a skills assessment and has two sessions with a career coach. The coach and the participant agree on a goal, such as "return as a backend developer" or "return to a project manager role". By the end of week 2 the participant has a short learning plan and an updated resume.

### Phase 2: Refresh (weeks 3 to 6)

The participant refreshes the skills she needs. This may be a technology orientation track, a self-paced course or practice projects. A mentor is assigned in week 3 and meets the participant every week for thirty minutes. Mentor Circles run every two weeks so participants can learn from each other.

### Phase 3: Project (weeks 7 to 14)

The participant joins a partner company on a defined project. The project phase lasts eight weeks. The hiring manager sets clear weekly goals, and the participant reports progress in a short weekly check-in. The career coach joins a monthly review to remove blockers.

### Phase 4: Review (weeks 15 and 16)

The participant, the hiring manager and the coach hold a review meeting. They look at what was delivered, what the participant learned and what should happen next. Possible outcomes are an offer to continue, an extension of the project, or support to apply to other companies.

## Who does what

- **Participant:** attends check-ins, completes the learning plan, delivers the project goals and asks for help early.
- **Career coach:** guides the plan, joins the monthly review and supports the participant through difficult moments.
- **Mentor:** a senior professional who offers advice, feedback and encouragement. The mentor does not assign work.
- **Hiring manager:** defines the project, gives feedback every week and writes the final review.
- **Resourcing specialist:** connects participants with partner companies and helps match skills with projects.

## Weekly rhythm during the project phase

Monday: the hiring manager shares the goals for the week. Wednesday: the participant meets her mentor. Friday: the participant sends a short written update to the hiring manager and the coach.

## What success looks like

A returnship is successful when the participant has delivered her project goals, received written feedback from the hiring manager, and has a clear next step, whether that is a role with the partner company or another opportunity.

## Getting help

Participants who face a problem at any phase should contact the career coach first. Hiring managers should contact the resourcing specialist.
