# Jobs and registration

SYNTHETIC training document based on public information from hersecondinnings.com. Not an official HSI document.

## Registering as a candidate

Candidates register on the HSI website. Registration is free. After registering, candidates sign in to the candidate portal.

## Finding jobs

The jobs section lists openings. Candidates can explore all jobs and apply from there.

## Registering as an employer

Employers create an account, use the Post a Job option to publish openings, and review applicants from their account.

## Mentors

Women who want to guide returners can sign up as mentors on the mentors page.
