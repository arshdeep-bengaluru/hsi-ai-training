# Mentors and community

SYNTHETIC training document based on public information from hersecondinnings.com. Not an official HSI document.

## Mentors

Mentors are experienced professionals who volunteer to guide women returning to work. The mentors page lists them and explains how to sign up.

## Mentor Circles

Mentor Circles are small-group sessions where returners learn from mentors and from each other.

## Community team

HSI's community manager plans returnship programs and boot camps. The resourcing specialist works between returning women and hiring managers.
